The [[Wyrm]]’s collection of Inscribed [[Soul|souls]] and their accumulated knowledge.

## From the current canon

> <strong>The Library does not reveal what it is. It reveals what each visitor is capable of perceiving.</strong> Books and pages are the Oathbreaker's subjective perception; souls are not literally turned into pages. Only the Stewards' race has the established capacity to make physical objects out of souls.
