The [[Ancient King]]’s generational pact for the post-death [[Inscripture]] of royal descendants.

## From the current canon

> The <strong>Bloodline Testament</strong> is the Ancient King's agreement with the Wyrm, pledging his descendants to Inscripture upon death. It is an agreement, not a mortal spell compelling the Wyrm. The abandoned idea that every descendant must personally pair Spiritus with the Wyrm to finalize it is <strong>not canon</strong>.
