The [[Wyrm]]’s single physical manifestation: a sword that serves as [[Oathbreaker]]’s conduit to the Ancestral Domain, letting him invoke ancestors of his bloodline.

## From the current canon

> The Oathbreaker unknowingly descends from an illegitimate surviving branch of the Ancient Kingdom's royal line. His Codex is a manifestation of the Wyrm and conduit to the Ancestral Domain, not a container for souls. Through <strong>Domain Invocation</strong>, he requests a voluntary <strong>Ancestral Blessing</strong>; Inscribed ancestors retain Sovereignty and may refuse. The Codex is a signature weapon outside the normal deck.
