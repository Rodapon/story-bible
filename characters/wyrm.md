An ancient knowledge-seeking anomaly that makes [[Binding Contract|Binding Contracts]] and maintains the [[Library]].

## From the current canon

> The Wyrm is an Ancient Anomaly with a vast Library of knowledge. It occasionally offers civilizations, groups or bloodlines a continuing communal afterlife through Inscripture in exchange for the knowledge of their members. It acquires knowledge passively; not all knowledge is equally valuable, and it does not pursue only exceptional knowledge. It is indifferent to a civilization's survival. The Wyrm's deeper motives and <strong>the game</strong> remain unexplained.
