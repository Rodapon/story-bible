A primeval insectoid entity whose [[Preservation]] allows chosen individuals to be Restored after death.

## From the current canon

> <strong>Preservation</strong> is placed by the Queen on a living being and takes precedence over Inscripture at death; it allows <strong>Restoration</strong>. Preservation and Inscripture are different powers, not a strength ranking.
