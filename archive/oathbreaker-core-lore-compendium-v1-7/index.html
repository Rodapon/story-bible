2. NEWLY ESTABLISHED LORE

  • Faith is the supreme cosmic principle, and Will is its inward-directed expression.
  • The Archivist retained knowledgeable souls long before the ancient kingdom; this pre-Veil collection is The Library.
  • The Queen creates soulless insectoid life and perceives through her colony using Collective Sight — The Thousand Eyes.
  • The ancient kingdom studied Will for generations after the Archivist revealed it to an early king.
  • X deliberately exterminated the kingdom's Will practitioners during the initial modern Rift chaos to suppress their teachings.
  • The Sovereign Veil hides the Archivist and Ancestral Domain from X; its bloodline-only Retention filter reduces the chance of discovery.
  • The Veil also incidentally hides the Domain from the Queen, who cannot restore already-retained souls regardless.
  • During the Oathbreaker's breach incident, the Queen's pre-death Preservation overrides his pending Retention and provokes the Archivist to manifest as The Codex.
  • The Oathbreaker himself causes the breach through his uncontrolled actions; his guilt is real.
  • His retained family silently maintains his helmet and sword and remains among his few sources of genuine external Faith.

3. COSMIC PRINCIPLES AND HIERARCHY

Order Principle / entity Innate scope Limitation
1 Faith Genuine belief amplifies a soul's existing abilities. Cannot manufacture an innate ability or be forced into authenticity.
2 Will Inward Faith amplifies the believer's own soul. Not an independent force or universal soul-command ability.
3 Queen Creates life, preserves and restores souls; acts through her colony. Cannot restore already-retained souls or perceive through the Veil.
4 Archivist Retains real, conscious souls indefinitely. Domain-bound; cannot restore souls or independently wage worldly campaigns.
5 The Codex Archivist's physical manifestation and Dominion conduit. Cannot act without a wielder.
6 Oathbreaker Mortal heir wielding Ancestral Dominion, amplified by Will and genuine Faith. Limited to authorized inheritance; cannot compel Faith.
  • Ranking note: This is a hierarchy of cosmic scope and condition precedence, not a guaranteed combat ranking.
  • X / Coalition: Unranked against the Queen; its power varies with genuine external Faith and soul consumption.

Faith and Will [Established]

  • External Faith: Belief freely generated by another soul; deception can induce genuine belief, but coercion cannot fabricate it.
  • Will: Genuine self-belief, conviction, and acceptance; unstable in someone overwhelmed by guilt.
  • Shared rule: Both amplify capabilities already belonging to the recipient, rather than creating arbitrary powers.
  • All soul-bearing entities: The Queen, Archivist, X, and mortals are subject to Faith.
  • Soulless insects: Cannot supply Faith or be controlled through X's ordinary soul-targeting methods.

5. ANCIENT ENTITIES AND THEIR DOMAINS

The Queen [Established]

Ability Function
Life creation Generates living, soulless insectoid subjects.
Preservation Establishes a pre-death condition protecting a soul's capacity for restoration.
Restoration Returns a preserved soul to embodied life.
Collective Sight — The Thousand Eyes Perceives distant environments through biologically connected insects.
Champion selection Opportunistically preserves useful individuals for an undisclosed agenda.
  • Colony model: Like ants, subjects follow primitive instincts and independently forage, scout, and survive.
  • Passive awareness: Insects relay basic impressions of movement, threats, and unusual events without requiring the Queen's constant attention.
  • Focused sight: She can concentrate on an individual insect's sensory experience.
  • Collective observation: Many insects survey different regions, helping her identify possible Champions.
  • Limits: Scouts must physically reach an area, and their senses cannot read Faith, reveal hidden souls, or penetrate the Sovereign Veil.
  • Independence: Preservation does not force a Champion to believe in or obey her.
  • Unknown agenda: Her motives are neither confirmed benevolent nor confirmed malicious.

The Archivist [Established]

Form / domain Function
Native Retention Selects and stores knowledgeable souls as conscious individuals.
The Library Original pre-Veil collection spanning ancient civilizations.
Ancestral Domain Concealed kingdom-related spiritual inheritance established through the final contingency.
Old-man manifestation Indirectly guides mortals through knowledge and direction.
The Codex manifestation Physically channels inherited Dominion through another person's actions.
  • The Library: Its original souls and knowledge survive the Veil, but remain inaccessible to the Oathbreaker.
  • Native limitation: Retention preserves consciousness and memory, not the ability to return to life.
  • Worldly limitation: The Archivist cannot independently wield its sword, command armies, or create living bodies.
  • Relationship to Veil: The artificial overlay limits its selections and access without wounding or erasing its innate nature.

6. THE SOVEREIGN VEIL [ESTABLISHED]

Layer Purpose / effect
Primary concealment Hide the Archivist, Ancestral Domain, and their spiritual connections from X.
Bloodline-only Retention Restrict future selection to designated royal descendants, reducing detectable activity.
Inheritance access Restrict royal heirs to authorized bloodline souls, not The Library or the entire kingdom.
Death-triggered transfer Route eligible bloodline souls into Retention upon death unless an earlier incompatible Preservation condition takes priority.
Incidental concealment Prevent the Queen from perceiving the Domain, despite her not being the intended target.
Structural defects Unfinished, untested, without redundancy, tested failover, or completed reversal spell.
  • Origin: The reigning king, royal family, and surviving scholars enacted the Veil only during the kingdom's final hours under X's genocide.
  • Emergency collection: Dying citizens, including some non-bloodline individuals, were retained during the final contingency.
  • Later rule: Subsequent Retention is limited to eligible royal bloodline descendants, and the Oathbreaker cannot access all emergency-retained citizens.
  • Design intent: Concealment and restricted collection protect against X's destructive Consumption, not against the Queen.
  • No bypass: Stronger Will does not automatically undo the Veil or grant access to The Library.
  • Queen distinction: Even without concealment, she cannot restore souls that have already entered Retention.

7. CORE STORY — THE RIFT CONVERGENCE

Earth, the Rifts, and the Accord [Established]

Decades before the game, Rifts emerged and made ordinary Earth the crossroads of multiple worlds. Known modern Rifts lead to Earth rather than directly linking foreign worlds to one another. The civilizations exchanged technology, magic, culture, and resources, but growing tensions eventually escalated into war. The Accord formed to preserve coexistence, even as some of its leaders were secretly manipulated by X / the Coalition.

Rifts are pathways, not the source of Faith. They also expose civilizations to ancient beings capable of exploiting spiritual belief. The Champions' task is to confront threats across connected worlds, not indiscriminately close every Rift. A rare, human-sized precursor Rift connecting the ancient kingdom to the Archivist predates the modern Earth-centered network [Working].

X and the manufactured salvation [Established]

X / the Coalition consists of a small, exceptionally powerful group associated with a founding Rift world. It engineers wars, suffering, and dependence, then presents itself as salvation so desperate populations freely direct genuine Faith toward it. This strengthens the recipients' existing abilities; X can influence and alter believers, but cannot simply force authentic belief into existence.

Soul Consumption permanently destroys souls and prolongs the Coalition's lifespan. Their own world's supply is insufficient, motivating expansion across Rifts. Their apparently utopian homeworld is constructed from the corpses of followers. X publicly condemns Will as foul, selfish, and dangerous because self-directed Faith undermines dependence on external saviors.

The ancient kingdom and its destruction [Established]

Long before the modern Rifts, the Archivist appeared as an old man to an ancient human ruler. Through knowledge and direction, he revealed Will, which already existed as a possibility within souls; he did not invent it. Generations of royal scholars studied Will and soul structures, developing the inherited authority called Ancestral Dominion.

X saw this civilization as an existential and ideological threat. If ordinary individuals could amplify their own souls through Will, X's cultivated monopoly on salvation and external Faith could collapse. When the first modern Rifts caused widespread chaos, X immediately launched a deliberate genocide against the kingdom and those who practiced, taught, or protected Will. The genocide preceded the Sovereign Veil; X did not attack because of the Veil.

As extermination reached the kingdom's final hours, the reigning king, royal family, and surviving scholars devised an emergency spiritual contingency. They used the Archivist's Retention to save dying souls and imposed the unfinished Sovereign Veil to conceal the Archivist and Ancestral Domain from X. Restricting subsequent collection to the royal bloodline reduced spiritual traces that might reveal the hiding place. The experiment lacked redundancy, a tested failover, and a completed reversal spell. Scattered descendants survived without knowing their ancestry.

The Queen, the colony, and the Champions [Established direction]

The Accord encounters a strange insectoid colony whose subjects have no souls and therefore resist X's ordinary soul-based methods. A truce against the colony brings together Champions from formerly hostile worlds. The Queen's soulless creations form a distributed sensory network, Collective Sight — The Thousand Eyes, through which she searches the physical world for useful individuals.

The Queen's choices are opportunistic, and her real agenda remains unknown. She Preserves selected living souls and can later Restore them, creating the game's rebirth loop. Linked Champions can recover from near death; when the whole party falls and the field connection fails, preserved souls return to living bodies at the colony. Preservation does not require devotion, grant the Queen automatic Faith, or imply moral worthiness.

8. CORE CHARACTER STORY — THE OATHBREAKER

Identity, oath, and guilt [Established]

The Oathbreaker is an experienced knight descended from a forgotten, illegitimate branch of the ancient royal house. Unaware of his ancestry, he swore to defend his hometown and the people who trusted him. He earned genuine Faith from only a handful of living people he helped during his service and travels.

His title reflects a real catastrophe, not a misunderstanding that will later absolve him. His actions caused the breach that killed his family and devastated his home, even though he never intended to abandon his oath. He seeks atonement and wants to prove that his promise was not willingly forsaken.

The sword manifests during the breach [Established]

During the unfolding incident, the Queen selects the living knight and establishes a Preservation Condition on his soul. Her Collective Sight gives her a way to discover him through scouts, although whether a specific insect witnessed the selection is [Open]. Because he is an eligible royal descendant, the Veil would ordinarily route his soul into the Archivist's Retention when he dies. The Queen's earlier Preservation threatens that pending transfer.

The Archivist reacts by manifesting as The Codex. This is not an heirloom inherited from the Oathbreaker's father and not an independent combatant. The manifestation exposes the knight's dormant Ancestral Dominion, granting him access to his authorized inheritance through a physical conduit. It does not create Will or grant access to The Library.

The knight then acts recklessly with power he does not understand. His own use of the sword and Dominion causes the breach; neither the Queen nor the Archivist simply possesses him or commits the act in his place. The precise mechanism of the rupture remains [Open].

The family, silence, and Faith [Established]

His parents, sisters, and other eligible blood relatives die in the catastrophe and enter Retention under the Veil. They remain conscious, remember their lives, and cannot be restored by the Queen. The Oathbreaker himself remains eligible for the Queen's future Restoration because she established Preservation while he was alive.

His family never speaks to him, but silently polishes his helmet, sharpens and cleans his sword, and tends his equipment. This explains the unusually clean helmet and the bright, maintained end of his otherwise worn blade. Their continued genuine Faith is not proof that they forgive him or believe he is innocent. Most other retained souls resent being compelled to serve him; Dominion commands action, never authentic belief.

The Oathbreaker has a handsome, mature face, golden eyes, and a white-grey buzz cut, without scars or disfigurement. He is physically able to remove his helmet but refuses to do so in anyone else's sight because of shame. His inner Will fluctuates with guilt, while rare genuine external Faith comes from his family and the few living people who still believe in him.

Character thesis: He can command the dead, yet cannot compel the belief of a single soul. The people who know him best say nothing, but care for him anyway.

11. CANON GUARDRAILS

  • Faith is primary; Will is inward Faith, not a separate energy source.
  • The Queen creates soulless insect life, observes through it, and preserves/restores eligible souls; she does not create souls from nothing.
  • Collective Sight requires physical scouts and does not reveal the concealed Ancestral Domain.
  • The Library is the Archivist's original pre-Veil collection, not an inheritance the Oathbreaker can command.
  • The Veil conceals the Archivist and Domain from X and restricts future Retention to the bloodline; it does not wound the Archivist.
  • Emergency-retained kingdom citizens and later bloodline-only descendants are distinct categories.
  • Preservation before death takes precedence over the Oathbreaker's pending Retention, but never restores already-retained souls.
  • The sword manifests during the breach in response to the threatened transfer; it is not a family heirloom.
  • The Oathbreaker causes the breach, and his family is genuinely faithful but silent.
  • X / Coalition and the Accord are distinct; Earth is the modern Rift hub.
