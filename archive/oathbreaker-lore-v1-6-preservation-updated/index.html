THE RIFT CONVERGENCE

Core Lore & Oathbreaker Character Bible — Consolidated v1.6

Purpose: Compact, AI-readable reference for narrative, character design, cards, and gameplay.
Source order: Existing story.md baseline first; developments from subsequent lore discussions second.
Revision 1.1: Confirms the Archivist manifests as the Soulbound Sword during the breach, in response to the Queen’s earlier-acting preservation condition. Removes the earlier family-heirloom sword proposal. Clarifies the Archivist’s indirect, domain-centered influence.
Status key: [Established] = directly specified or accepted direction; [Working] = useful connective explanation awaiting final approval; [Open] = unanswered.
Revision: v1.2 — X’s ideological motive, targeted genocide, and the king’s final Veil contingency are established.

Revision v1.4: Clarifies the Sovereign Veil was specifically engineered to hide the Archivist and Ancestral Domain from X. Restricting future Retention to the royal bloodline reduces detectable activity. The Queen is incidentally blinded to the Domain, but cannot restore retained souls regardless.

Names: The Coalition is the working name for X; the Accord is the interworld alliance, not X. Archivist, Sovereign Veil, and Ancestral Dominion are working terms used consistently here.

PART I — WHAT EXISTED BEFORE

A. Original overarching story (from the earlier story file)

  • Rifts emerged decades ago. The connected civilizations formed the Accord, exchanged culture, magic, technology, and resources, and eventually descended into war.
  • X / the Coalition secretly controls key Accord leaders. It manufactures conflict so civilizations will willingly trust its promised salvation.
  • Faith makes the Coalition's members stronger and harder to kill, and gives them influence over believers. It cannot force genuine belief.
  • Soul Consumption extends X's lifespan by permanently destroying souls. Its home world's false utopia is literally built from the remains of consumed followers.
  • A newly encountered insectoid Queen rules a colony of soulless subjects. Those subjects resist the Coalition's usual soul-targeting methods.
  • The Queen preserves and rebirths heroes. Their linked souls explain near-death recovery, party wipes, and the roguelike rebirth loop.
  • The Champions travel between worlds, uncover manipulation, and struggle to protect coexistence.
  • The original Oathbreaker concept was a disgraced knight seeking atonement and insisting his oath was not intentionally abandoned. His Soulbound Sword involves unwilling souls whom he compels to serve.

B. Previously clarified world direction

  • Earth is an ordinary human world transformed into the crossroads of the modern Rift network; every known modern Rift leads to Earth, not directly from one foreign world to another.
  • The Champions' objective is generally to resolve threats across Rifts, not routinely close the Rifts and sever connected communities.
  • The Coalition's world is a deceptive paradise; the Queen's world is the colony and resurrection/progression hub. These are distinct from Earth, the geographic and political hub.
  • Fantasy, artificial-intelligence, and spirit-centered worlds remain possible settings, not finalized worlds.

PART II — WHAT HAS NOW BEEN ADDED OR REFINED

  1. Faith is the primary cosmic power; Will is its inward, sub-cosmic expression, not a rival energy source.
  2. Faith empowers an existing soul and its innate abilities; Will strengthens the user's own soul through genuine self-belief. Neither grants every possible power.
  3. Ancient, soul-bearing entities obey Faith too. Rifts can expose civilizations to beings who exploit Faith, devotion, and spiritual vulnerability.
  4. The Queen is an ancient, abstract life-generating and life-restoring entity, with purposes beyond the heroes' understanding. She does not create souls out of nothing.
  5. Consume, Retain, Preserve, Restore are distinct operations. Only preserved souls can be restored.
  6. The Archivist is an ancient, conscious collector of unusually knowledgeable souls. Its old-man manifestation influences through knowledge and direction; its Soulbound Sword manifestation is wielded by another, not self-directed.
  7. The Archivist taught an ancient human king about Will, but did not create Will. The kingdom studied it over generations and developed Ancestral Dominion.
  8. X destroyed the ancient kingdom during the initial chaos of the modern Rift era. Its scholars activated the unfinished Sovereign Veil.
  9. The Veil is an artificial overlay designed foremost to conceal the Archivist and Ancestral Domain from X. It limits future Retention to the designated royal bloodline to reduce discovery risk, and restricts the Oathbreaker's access to his authorized inheritance. It does not injure or erase the Archivist's native ability.
  10. The Veil was untested, unfinished, built without redundancy, and had no completed reversal spell.
  11. During the breach incident, the Queen establishes Preservation on the living Oathbreaker before Retention can occur. Her condition overrides the pending ancestral transfer for his soul; the threatened Archivist responds by manifesting as the Soulbound Sword. This precedence is why the Queen outranks the Archivist in this hierarchy.
  12. The newly manifested Soulbound Sword becomes the conduit for the Oathbreaker’s awakened inheritance; his own actions cause the breach, kill his family, and earn him the title Oathbreaker. The Archivist does not directly act in the world for him.
  13. His deceased relatives are among his few genuine believers. They silently polish his helmet, sharpen his sword, and care for his equipment. Very few living people he helped also give him Faith.
  14. He can remove his helmet, but never does so in another person's sight because of his guilt.
  15. His Soulbound Sword is a signature-weapon slot, not an ordinary card that can vanish in a bloated late-game deck.

PART III — COSMIC ORDER & DEFINITIONS

Reading rule: The ordering below moves from cosmic principle → derived expression → ancient entities → manifestation → mortal inheritor. It is not a claim that a metaphysical law is a combatant.

1. FAITH — Primary cosmic principle [Established]

Faith is genuine belief directed by a soul toward a being, purpose, or oneself. It amplifies the recipient's soul and its existing capabilities. External Faith comes from other independent souls; it cannot be fabricated through coercion. The Coalition can manipulate circumstances to inspire devotion but cannot directly command authentic belief into existence.

Core law: Faith gives power; the nature of the recipient determines what that power can do. More believers do not create new innate abilities, and no unlimited Faith multiplier has been established.

2. WILL — Sub-cosmic offshoot of Faith [Established]

Will is Faith directed inward: sincere self-conviction, acceptance, and belief in one's own capacity. It amplifies one's own soul, but is volatile and demands discipline and efficient control.

Will is not: a second universal source; automatic resurrection; soul creation; unrestricted soul command; a method for bypassing the Veil. External Faith and Will can coexist, but they remain expressions of one cosmic principle.

3. THE QUEEN — Ancient life-generating/restoring entity [Established direction]

An abstract, insectoid matriarch whose innate power allows her to generate life and soulless insectoid subjects, preserve souls in a restorable state, and restore preserved souls to embodied life. Her own soul makes her subject to Faith. Her ordinary subjects are soulless and cannot supply soul-derived Faith or be controlled by X through its usual soul-targeting methods.

Scope: Unlike the Archivist, the Queen acts directly upon the living world through her colony, creation of living subjects, and restoration of Champions. She is not established as the creator of souls from nothing. Her choices serve her own game, not necessarily the Champions' interests.

Collective Sight — The Thousand Eyes [Established]

The Queen's soulless insectoid subjects operate as a distributed sensory colony, analogous to ants. Each creature has primitive instincts and can independently scout, navigate, forage, and survive, while retaining an innate biological sensory link to the Queen who created it. The link is an extension of her life-creation authority, not a separate cosmic force, soul possession, or Faith supplied by her subjects.

  • Passive awareness: Colony members transmit simple impressions of movement, threats, and unusual events. The Queen does not consciously experience every subject's full senses at all times.
  • Focused sight: She may concentrate through a particular subject and perceive its surroundings through that creature's sensory capabilities.
  • Collective observation: Many subjects can survey different places, giving her a distributed understanding of distant events and allowing her to search for potential Champions to Preserve and later Restore in pursuit of her own goals.

Limits: Her subjects must physically reach a location; she is not omniscient. Their eyes do not inherently read Faith, identify hidden souls, or pierce the Sovereign Veil. Physical observation of an individual is not itself Preservation and does not automatically establish a Preservation Condition. The Queen's subjects remain soulless and cannot generate genuine external Faith.

Precedence over the Archivist: Her Preservation Condition attaches to a living soul, before the death-triggered Retention Condition can complete. In the Oathbreaker's case, Preservation overrides the pending transfer. Her higher tier reflects priority of soul continuity and direct influence, not a claim that she can erase the Archivist's innate Retention or restore its already-retained souls.

4. THE ARCHIVIST — Ancient retainer of souls [Established direction]

An incomprehensibly old, conscious being whose innate sphere is the Domain of Retained Souls. For millennia before the kingdom, it elected unusually knowledgeable souls to retain as real, conscious individuals, with memory and capacity for Faith intact but no restorable continuity.

Mode of influence: The Archivist is not a world-intervening creator or commander like the Queen. Its domain is its domain; it cannot dispatch an army, remake living bodies, or directly wield its sword to act upon the world. When it appeared as the old man, it influenced mortals through knowledge and direction. As the Soulbound Sword, it depends on another being to wield it. These are channels of indirect influence, not proof of unrestricted physical agency.

The Library—the Archivist’s pre-Veil collection of selected, knowledgeable souls—still contains millennia of information. The Veil restricts new selections and what a royal heir can access; it does not destroy those souls or memories. Retention cannot restore an already-retained soul.

5. THE SOULBOUND SWORD — Manifestation of the Archivist [Established direction]

The Soulbound Sword is a physical manifestation of the Archivist, not a normal family heirloom or a separate weapon imprisoning a god. It first manifests in the Oathbreaker's timeline during the breach incident, as the Archivist's response to the Queen's Preservation Condition threatening the expected Retention of his bloodline soul. The old man was a different manifestation of the same being in the kingdom's ancient history.

Physical agency: The sword does not swing itself or directly intervene; its wielder influences how it acts. The Archivist can make contact and provide knowledge or direction, but the Oathbreaker must choose and perform his actions. The sword channels authorized Invocation and inherited Ancestral Dominion; it neither creates Dominion nor grants access to The Library or other unauthorized souls.

6. THE OATHBREAKER — Mortal royal descendant [Established direction]

A knight from a forgotten, illegitimate branch of the ancient royal line. His inherited Ancestral Dominion allows him to command eligible souls in the bloodline's authorized inheritance. Will enhances the efficiency of his personal authority; external Faith from rare genuine believers further amplifies him.

Key limitation: He cannot compel belief, access The Library, create souls, or restore souls retained in the sword.

How they influence reality [Established]

Being / form Sphere Mode of influence
Queen Living world and her colony Direct: generates insectoid life, directs soulless subjects, preserves and restores Champions.
Archivist Domain of Retained Souls Domain-bound: retains souls; cannot directly conduct worldly campaigns or wield the sword by itself.
Old-man manifestation Knowledge and direction Indirect: advises, reveals principles, guides choices; mortals act.
Soulbound Sword Physical conduit Wielder-directed: responds to a bearer; does not independently pursue battles.

PART IV — POWER SCALING TIERS

This is a narrative scale of supernatural scope, not a guaranteed one-on-one combat ranking. Faith and Will are above these tiers as operating principles, not fighters.

Tier Position Power and limitation
S — Ancient life authority The Queen Acts directly in the world through life creation, soulless subjects, and restoration; Preservation is established before death and overrides the Oathbreaker’s pending Retention. Above the Archivist by condition precedence and worldly reach, not omnipotence.
A — Ancient continuity authority The Archivist Vast domain-bound Retention and knowledge. Influences via teaching/direction or a wielder-driven manifestation; cannot directly intervene like the Queen or restore retained souls.
B — Wielder-driven conduit Soulbound Sword Manifested at the breach in answer to the Queen. Physical expression of the Archivist, not a second entity; action depends on the wielder.
C → potentially much higher Oathbreaker Initially an inefficient, dangerous user of Dominion. Growth comes from mastery of Will, controlled invocation, and genuine Faith—not endlessly adding souls.
Unranked / variable Coalition / X Members possess soul manipulation and consumption, amplified by mass Faith. Their combined strength may be immense, but there is no settled direct tier relative to the Queen.

Character progression: Early: crude invocations, unstable control. Mid: deliberate skills, better selection and efficiency. Late: sophisticated combinations and stronger Will; massive potential, never unrestricted access to pre-Veil souls. The sword must remain reliably accessible through its signature slot rather than competing with normal card draw.

PART V — SOUL CONTINUITY: THE DEFINING OPERATIONS

Operation Primary user What happens to the soul? Identity survives? Can it be restored?
CONSUME Coalition / X Spiritual continuity is destroyed for sustenance and lifespan. No No
RETAIN Archivist Original conscious soul persists in an enduring archive, but loses the continuity needed for future embodiment. Yes No
PRESERVE Queen Original soul is held in a restorable condition; identity and vital continuity persist. Yes Yes
RESTORE Queen An already preserved soul returns to a living body. Yes Completed

Law of Spiritual Continuity: Only preserved souls can be restored. Retained ≠ preserved. A retained soul is a person, not a memory copy, but its lost restorable condition cannot simply be reconstructed by Faith or Will. No established method can turn an already-retained soul into a preserved soul.

Terminology note: The Queen’s soul-operation is PRESERVE → RESTORE. CONSUME refers to X’s destructive soul operation, not the Queen’s preservation process.

PART VI — CORE WORLD STORY (SEPARATE FROM THE OATHBREAKER)

Earth, Rifts, and the Accord [Established]

Modern Rifts appeared decades ago and connect other worlds through Earth, the shared crossroads. Earth became home to mixed populations, technologies, and beliefs. The Accord was founded to maintain coexistence; interworld tensions escalated into war. Heroes enter worlds to address crises, not simply seal every Rift.

Ancient exception [Working]: A rare, human-sized Rift linked the ancient kingdom with the Archivist centuries or millennia before the modern convergence. This precursor anomaly is distinct from the modern Earth-centered network.

Danger: A Rift is a pathway, not the origin of Faith. Some pathways expose ordinary worlds to extremely powerful entities capable of exploiting soul-based Faith, belief, and spiritual dependency.

X / the Coalition — The manufactured salvation [Established]

A small group of unusually powerful members of a founding world secretly manipulates Accord leaders. They orchestrate war, desperation, and promised rescue so populations choose to place Faith in them. Their abilities allow limited direct coercion of unwilling individuals, and much broader influence over genuine followers. Strong enough Faith can enable soul alteration and bodily transformation.

Faith sustains their strength; CONSUMPTION sustains their lifespan. Their own world's souls cannot replenish fast enough, so they expand their supply across worlds. Their seemingly perfect homeworld is built from the bodies of dead followers. They publicly condemn self-directed Will as dangerous or impure while cultivating dependence on their supposedly benevolent authority. Their individual identities and full goals remain open.

The Queen's colony and her game [Established direction]

A strange, primitive soulless insectoid colony is discovered through a Rift. The Coalition cannot ordinarily influence its subjects through Faith-based soul control. The Accord declares a truce to oppose the colony, allowing Champions from formerly hostile worlds to meet on that front.

Through Collective Sight (the Thousand Eyes), the Queen can scout the world through her soulless insectoid subjects and identify possible Champions without appearing in person. The Queen chooses Champions opportunistically: potential usefulness and chance encounters, not moral worthiness, shape whom she preserves. She is neither an unquestioned savior nor necessarily a villain. Her ultimate objective is still hidden. A preserved Champion can oppose her, doubt her, or deny her Faith; preservation does not force genuine belief.

She senses the Coalition's disappearance of consumed souls [original lore]. Her colony changes through unlocks and development, reflecting the run-to-run progression hub. Exactly how her colony obtains technology and what price the Champions pay remain [Open].

Champions and rebirth loop [Established gameplay fiction]

Champions' linked souls let nearby allies recover from near death. Being downed weakens the link; when the whole party falls, the field connection fails and the Queen restores preserved Champions at her colony, ending the run. The exact proximity, solo exceptions, and consent/ownership rules remain [Open].

The ancient human kingdom and X's attack [Established direction]

Long before the modern Accord, an ancient human ruler encountered the old-man manifestation of the Archivist. The Archivist, appearing as an old man, influenced the ruler through knowledge and direction: it revealed Will, a truth already latent in souls, and the royal line developed Ancestral Dominion. It did not directly shape the kingdom through a worldly army or restoration. Scholars studied Will and spiritual structures for generations.

Why X exterminated the kingdom [Established]

X regarded the kingdom’s mastery of Will as both an existential threat and ideological blasphemy. His doctrine condemned inward-directed Faith as foul, selfish, and dangerous, demanding that souls instead place genuine Faith in the saviors he represented. The kingdom demonstrated the opposite: individuals could amplify their own souls through Will, without surrendering their belief to X. If that knowledge spread, it could undermine his cultivated dependence, followers, and power.

When the first modern Rifts threw worlds into chaos, X wasted no time. He exploited the confusion to launch a sweeping genocide, deliberately killing those who practiced, embodied, taught, or safeguarded Will. This was an intentional eradication of a civilization and its spiritual knowledge, not a response to the Veil.

The final contingency [Established]

Only as the extermination overtook the kingdom did the surviving scholars, reigning king, and royal family propose and enact a last-ditch contingency: the Sovereign Veil. They sought to save what remained of the king’s bloodline and the kingdom’s spiritual inheritance from total erasure and hide the Archivist and the resulting Ancestral Domain from X. Using the Archivist’s innate Retention, they secured the dying kingdom’s souls in its domain, then imposed the Veil’s artificial bloodline selection, inheritance-access, and concealment conditions. The experiment was unfinished, unproven, without redundancy, and had no completed reversal spell. The royal line survived only in scattered descendants; the scholars could not undo what they had set in motion.

Sequence: Will threatens X’s ideology → Rift chaos → X’s targeted genocide → king, family, and scholars’ desperate proposal → Retention and activation of the Sovereign Veil.

The kingdom's defeat is recent relative to its age: ancient founding and research can be centuries or millennia old, while the Coalition's destruction occurred only decades before game present.

PART VII — THE SOVEREIGN VEIL: ARTIFICIAL OVERLAY

What it is: An experimental Will-assisted spiritual construct engineered by dying scholars around the Archivist’s innate Retention (whose pre-Veil collection is called The Library), not a new native power granted to him. Its primary purpose was to prevent X from discovering the Archivist and locating the Ancestral Domain, where the kingdom’s souls would otherwise be vulnerable to irreversible Consumption. Its bloodline-only collection rule was a secondary concealment measure: fewer outward selections meant fewer spiritual traces that might betray the hidden archive.

Layer Rule
Native Archivist May independently elect knowledgeable souls for retention.
Veil: future collection filter Future eligible transfers/selections are restricted to the designated royal bloodline to minimize detectable collection activity and the risk of X discovering the Archivist.
Veil: inheritance access filter The Oathbreaker may invoke only his authorized bloodline inheritance; he cannot read or wield the Archivist's older, unrelated collection.
Veil: concealment Specifically hides the Archivist, Ancestral Domain, and their spiritual connections from X. This also incidentally prevents the Queen from perceiving the Domain; it is not general immunity from all soul powers.
Transfer curse Eligible descendants are routed into Retention at death unless an earlier, incompatible preservation condition takes precedence.

Important historical distinction: At the kingdom's destruction, the scholars mass-retained its dying citizens, including non-bloodline individuals, as part of the final emergency. Those souls remain real and archived. But the Oathbreaker's current Dominion is restricted to the bloodline's authorized inheritance. The wider kingdom and the souls of The Library are not automatically his to command or consult. This strict access rule supersedes an earlier proposed broader command over all kingdom citizens.

Unintended consequence — the Queen: The Veil also conceals the Domain from her, although she was not the intended adversary. Even if she discovered the retained souls, Retention does not preserve their capacity for restoration. She cannot retroactively Preserve or Restore souls already under Retention. Her pre-death Preservation of the living Oathbreaker succeeds because it takes effect before his soul could enter Retention; it does not release any soul already archived.

Design flaws: unproven, unfinished, no redundancy, no tested failover, no completed reverse spell. The Veil did not wound the Archivist or delete its history; it limited behavior through an artificial condition layered over its nature. It was built to save an inheritance, not tested for a clash with another ancient being.

Boundary: Better Will cannot simply overwrite the Veil. Nor can the Oathbreaker use Dominion to seize any stranger's soul. The Archivist's ancient knowledge still exists, but the conduit does not expose it to him.

PART VIII — THE OATHBREAKER: PERSONAL CHARACTER STORY

Identity and oath [Established direction]

The Oathbreaker is an experienced knight descended from a forgotten bastard branch of the ancient royal house. He swore to protect his home and people. He really is responsible for the catastrophic breach that killed those he loved, even if he never intended to forsake his oath. His journey is one of atonement, not a revelation that he was entirely innocent.

Visual identity: worn armor, clean polished helmet, maintained sword, golden eyes, white-grey short buzz cut, handsome mature face with no scar required. He can take off the helmet but never removes it where others can see him, because he is ashamed of the man beneath it.

The sword’s manifestation — During the breach [Established]

The Oathbreaker did not inherit the dormant Soulbound Sword from his father. During the breach incident, the Queen’s Preservation Condition targeted his living soul. That pre-death condition threatened the Veil-imposed Retention Transfer that would otherwise claim him at death. The Archivist responded by manifesting as the Soulbound Sword at that moment. The sword's appearance was a reaction to threatened retention, not a gift for virtue or a weapon the Archivist could use independently.

The Archivist’s physical influence is indirect: the old man gives knowledge and direction; the sword requires a wielder to choose and act. The Domain remains its true sphere of operation. The Queen, by contrast, shapes the living world through her subjects and restoration.

The Queen’s condition — Why Preservation overrides Retention [Established]

Preservation is established while the subject is still alive, not when the subject dies. The Queen places a Preservation Condition on a living soul, protecting its capacity for restoration if death occurs later; she cannot retroactively preserve a soul that has already entered the Archivist’s Retention.

  1. During the unfolding breach incident, the Queen elects the living Oathbreaker for her own reasons and preserves his soul while he is alive. Her Collective Sight provides an established means to discover him through a nearby scout, but whether a scout witnessed the exact selection moment remains [Open].
  2. The Veil’s Ancestral Transfer would ordinarily retain his eligible bloodline soul upon death. The Queen’s earlier Preservation Condition overrides that pending claim; it does not rewrite the Archivist’s innate Retention.
  3. Threatened by the lost claim, the Archivist awakens and manifests into the Soulbound Sword. He recognizes the interference, not necessarily the Queen’s identity or intention.
  4. Contact with this new manifestation exposes the Oathbreaker’s inherited Ancestral Dominion. The Archivist does not give him Will or create his bloodline gift.
  5. The Oathbreaker himself wields the sword; his choices and uncontrolled use of newly accessible power cause the breach catastrophe.
  6. When he eventually dies, the already-established Preservation Condition means his soul is preserved, not retained, and the Queen may restore him. The ancestral transfer rule remains part of the unfinished overlay, but does not succeed while preservation has precedence.

Why the Queen outranks the Archivist: Preservation acts before retention, determining the soul’s restorable continuity while it is still living. This priority of conditions, together with her direct influence on living reality, establishes the tier ordering. It does not imply that the Queen can revive souls already retained by the Archivist.

The awakening, the breach, and the family [Established core; exact cause Open]

The Archivist’s sword manifestation makes the Oathbreaker’s dormant Ancestral Dominion accessible amid the incident. He is responsible for causing the breach through his own actions, rather than being possessed or forced by the Archivist. The exact physical and spiritual mechanics of the rupture remain [Open]. Do not invent a settled technical explanation that exonerates him.

His parents, sisters, and eligible blood-related townspeople die in the disaster. Their souls pass into the Archivist’s Retention under the bloodline condition, retaining memories, identity, consciousness, and potential Faith—but not restorability. The Queen’s separate condition saves the Oathbreaker from joining them in permanent retention.

The silent family and genuine Faith [Established direction]

Unlike many resentful bloodline ancestors, his immediate family continues to believe in him. They do not speak. Instead, they quietly polish his helmet, sharpen and clean his sword, and tend his gear. This supplies the lore behind the cleaner blade tip and helmet against the worn armor. Their silence does not prove forgiveness or innocence; their care expresses real Faith he cannot command into existence.

A very small number of living people whom he saved in early knighthood and later travels also genuinely believe in him. Most other souls serve under Dominion, not loyalty. Faith from these few people amplifies his soul; Will is his unstable inward belief. These are not different energy types.

Signature weapon and game identity [Established design direction]

The Soulbound Sword occupies a dedicated signature-weapon slot, always reliably available regardless of normal draw pile/deck bloat. Cards invoke eligible ancestral techniques, movement, defense, and attacks on the hex board. Growth is through refined Will, better invocation efficiency, positioning, and meaningful soul interactions—not simply accumulating unlimited new souls or arbitrary damage multipliers.

Character thesis: He can command countless inherited voices, yet cannot compel the belief of even one. The dead who know him best say nothing, but care for him anyway.

PART X — NON-NEGOTIABLE LORE RULES

  • Faith is primary; Will is inward Faith. Neither is a free-form spell system.
  • Faith empowers, but does not manufacture innate talents. Genuine belief cannot be forced.
  • The Queen is above the Archivist in the chosen hierarchy, but neither is outside Faith.
  • The Queen directly affects the world via her soulless subjects, life generation, and restoration; Collective Sight lets her perceive through the subjects without granting omniscience or bypassing the Veil; she is not known to create souls from nothing. The Archivist’s domain is the domain: he influences indirectly through the old man’s knowledge/direction and a sword controlled by its wielder.
  • Consume destroys; Retain endures without rebirth; Preserve keeps rebirth possible. Only the preserved can be restored.
  • The Archivist manifests as the Soulbound Sword during the breach incident in direct response to the Queen’s threatened overwrite of the pending Retention Condition; it was not inherited as a dormant sword. The old man was an earlier manifestation of the same entity.
  • The Veil is an untested overlay, not an innate cosmic law; it did not wound the Archivist. Concealing the Archivist and Domain from X is its central purpose; bloodline-only Retention limits discovery, while the Queen’s inability to see the Domain is incidental.
  • Oathbreaker has bloodline-gated access only, not free access to the Archivist's millennia-old unrelated collection.
  • The Queen’s Preservation Condition precedes and overrides the Oathbreaker’s death-triggered Retention Condition; this is why the Queen outranks the Archivist. The Queen’s selection triggers the Archivist’s sword manifestation, not the creation of inherited Dominion.
  • Oathbreaker caused the breach; his family genuinely believes in him but never speaks; he chooses to hide his face.
  • X is the Coalition, not the Accord. Modern Rifts converge on Earth, and the Rifts themselves are not Faith's origin.

PART XI — TEN QUESTIONS TO RESOLVE NEXT

  1. Coalition / X: Who exactly founded X, how many members exist, and do they all agree that harvesting Faith justifies soul consumption?
  2. Coalition / X: How did X identify the kingdom’s Will practitioners, and how thoroughly did he suppress their teachings after the genocide?
  3. Coalition / X: Does X know the Archivist survives, and could discovering the Veil let it exploit the bloodline or the archived souls?
  4. Coalition / X: What does X actually offer followers that inspires genuine Faith, and what events could convincingly break that trust?
  5. The Queen: What is her true objective in preserving Champions, and what does she gain from repeated restoration?
  6. The first encounter: Did a colony scout directly witness the Oathbreaker during the unfolding breach, and what specifically led the Queen to select him?
  7. The Archivist: Did the Archivist accept the Veil willingly, endure it against its will, or lack the opportunity to resist the kingdom's dying scholars?
  8. The Veil: Is its bloodline filter permanent in practice, or could a future researcher discover a dangerous bypass despite the missing reversal spell?
  9. The breach: What exact action did the Oathbreaker take after the sword manifested, what opened or failed, and who survived to name him an Oathbreaker?
  10. The inheritance: Which souls qualify as bloodline-accessible, and how many unrelated fallen-kingdom citizens remain archived but permanently inaccessible to him?

One-sentence premise: Across an Earth-centered network of Rifts, Faith empowers entities that can exploit believers; the Queen’s pre-death Preservation overrides a royal Retention curse, provoking the domain-bound Archivist to manifest as the wielder-driven Soulbound Sword during a breach caused by an oath-bound knight who must then seek atonement.
