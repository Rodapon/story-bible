THE RIFT CONVERGENCE

Core World Codex

Core Story, Ancient Entities & Cosmic Principles — v1.1

Scope: World-level story, factions, ancient entities unrelated to the Oathbreaker's personal lineage, and universal cosmic laws. The Ancient Kingdom, Archivist, The Codex, Sovereign Veil, Ancestral Domain, The Library, and Ancestral Dominion belong in the separate Oathbreaker Lore volume.

Canon priority: Only X / the Coalition harnesses Faith. Will is an independent inward power, not a subdivision of Faith. The Queen's powers are innate.

Status: Established = accepted canon; Working = not finalized; Open = unresolved.

I. CORE STORY

The Rift Convergence

  • Rifts appeared decades before the game's present, connecting otherwise isolated worlds through Earth, the central crossroads of the modern network.
  • Contact enabled exchanges of culture, technology, magic, and resources, but escalating rivalries led to interworld wars.
  • The Accord formed to preserve coexistence among connected civilizations; influential leaders were secretly manipulated by the Coalition.
  • Heroes travel into connected worlds to resolve crises, not necessarily to close every Rift.

X and Manufactured Salvation

  • X / the Coalition is a small group of ancient, powerful beings associated with a founding Rift world.
  • The Coalition secretly engineers war, suffering, and instability, then presents itself as salvation to cultivate genuine Faith in its members.
  • Faith amplifies the Coalition's existing supernatural capabilities, but genuine belief cannot simply be commanded into existence.
  • Soul Consumption irreversibly destroys souls to sustain the Coalition's longevity.
  • Its apparent utopia is built from the physical remains of dead followers, concealing exploitation beneath a promise of paradise.
  • X condemns Will as impure, selfish, or dangerous because independent spiritual strength threatens the Coalition's ideology of dependence.

The Queen's Colony

  • A Rift reveals an ancient insectoid Queen and her colony of soulless subjects.
  • The colony resists the Coalition's ordinary soul-targeting methods, and the Accord declares a truce to oppose it.
  • The Queen's motivations are unknown; she selects potentially useful Champions for her own purposes rather than on moral merit.
  • The Queen establishes Preservation while a selected subject is alive, enabling eventual Restoration if the subject dies.
  • Her colony functions as the hub to which preserved Champions can return following a failed expedition.

The Queen

  • The Queen is an ancient, abstract insectoid matriarch with innate powers over life creation, Preservation, and Restoration.
  • Her own soul does not make her a Faith user under the current canon; only the Coalition harnesses Faith.
  • Her ordinary insectoid subjects are soulless and therefore cannot grant Faith or be targeted by the Coalition's usual soul-based methods.
  • She does not have an established ability to create souls from nothing.

III. COSMIC PRINCIPLES & SPIRITUAL LAWS

Fundamental Terms

Keyword Definition Limitation
Faith Genuine belief that the Coalition alone harnesses to amplify its existing powers. Cannot be forced into authenticity or automatically grant new innate abilities.
Will Independent inward conviction that strengthens one's own soul and existing capabilities. Not a form of Faith; does not automatically grant resurrection or arbitrary supernatural abilities.
Soul A person's real spiritual identity and consciousness. Its survival and ability to return to life are separate questions.
Spiritual continuity Persistence of spiritual identity and, where protected, the possibility of future embodiment. Identity can persist without restorability.
Innate authority An entity's native supernatural capacity, determined by its nature. Faith does not automatically grant abilities outside the user's nature.

Governing Laws

  • Only the Coalition harnesses Faith. Others may possess souls or genuine convictions without receiving Faith-based supernatural amplification.
  • Will and Faith are distinct. Will is self-directed spiritual strength, not inward Faith under the latest retcon.
  • Preservation must be established while the subject is alive. It cannot be applied retroactively to a soul already under Retention or destroyed by Consumption.
  • Restoration requires prior Preservation. A conscious soul is not necessarily restorable.
  • The Queen's Collective Sight is biological, not omniscient. It depends on her living scouts and their sensory reach.
