THE RIFT CONVERGENCE

Codex of the Sovereign Veil — Categorized Keyword Glossary

Version: 1.1
Reference: The Rift Convergence: Codex of the Sovereign Veil, v1.7
Purpose: Quick-reference terms for writing, gameplay design, and AI-assisted development. Definitions reflect established lore unless explicitly marked [Descriptive / not official].

I. Cosmic Principles & Spiritual Laws

Keyword Definition
Faith Coalition-exclusive supernatural amplification drawn from genuine belief directed toward X or its members.
Will Independent inward spiritual conviction and self-mastery; not Faith received from others.
External Faith Belief harnessed exclusively by X / the Coalition; it cannot be forced into existence.
Soul A real individual's spiritual identity and consciousness, capable of retaining memory and belief.
Spiritual Continuity Persistence of a soul's identity; restorable continuity additionally permits future embodiment.
Consumption X's destructive soul operation, permanently eliminating identity and spiritual continuity.
Retention The Archivist's operation that keeps a conscious soul indefinitely but without restorability.
Preservation The Queen's condition established while a subject is alive, maintaining the soul's capacity for later restoration.
Restoration The Queen's return of an already-preserved soul to a living body.
Condition Precedence An existing pre-death Preservation Condition takes priority over the Oathbreaker's pending death-triggered Retention.

Retcon rule: Only X / the Coalition harnesses Faith. The Queen, Archivist, Oathbreaker, and their subjects do not receive Faith-based amplification. Will is separate from Coalition Faith.

Core rule: Consume destroys; Retain endures without rebirth; Preserve maintains the possibility of Restore.

II. Ancient Entities & Their Powers

Keyword Definition
The Queen Ancient, soul-bearing insectoid entity with innate authority over life creation, Preservation, and Restoration.
The Archivist Ancient, soul-bearing entity whose innate domain is the Retention of conscious souls.
Collective Sight — The Thousand Eyes The Queen's ability to perceive through biologically linked, soulless insectoid subjects.
Passive Awareness Basic sensory impressions sent to the Queen by her colony.
Focused Sight The Queen's concentrated perception through a particular subject's senses.
Collective Observation Distributed scouting by many insectoid subjects across physically reachable locations.
Old-Man Manifestation The Archivist's historical form used to impart knowledge and direction to an ancient ruler.
Soulbound Sword The Archivist's physical manifestation during the breach; it requires an external wielder to act.
The Library The Archivist's original pre-Veil collection of exceptionally knowledgeable retained souls.

III. The Sovereign Veil & Ancestral Systems

Keyword Definition
Sovereign Veil Unfinished artificial overlay designed to hide the Archivist and Ancestral Domain from X.
Ancestral Domain Concealed spiritual inheritance associated with the fallen kingdom and the Veil.
Ancestral Dominion Inherited royal authority allowing the Oathbreaker to command eligible retained ancestral souls.
Royal Bloodline The designated lineage eligible for future Veil-restricted Retention.
Ancestral Transfer The Veil's death-triggered routing of eligible descendants into Retention, unless a prior condition takes precedence.
Bloodline Selection Filter Veil restriction on future Retention, intended to reduce activity that could expose the Archivist.
Inheritance Access Filter Veil restriction preventing the Oathbreaker from invoking The Library or other unauthorized souls.
Veil Concealment Hides the Archivist, Domain, and their spiritual connections from X; incidentally obscures them from the Queen.
Final Contingency The dying king, royal family, and scholars' emergency effort to preserve their spiritual inheritance from X.

IV. Worlds, Rifts & Political Factions

Keyword Definition
The Rift Convergence The modern emergence of interworld Rifts centered on Earth.
Rifts Pathways connecting otherwise separate worlds through Earth; not the source of Faith.
Earth Crossroads of the modern Rift network and a hub of interworld contact.
The Accord Political alliance formed to maintain coexistence between connected civilizations.
X / The Coalition Ancient antagonistic group that engineers conflict to cultivate Faith and consumes souls for longevity.
Coalition Homeworld Deceptively utopian world built from the remains of consumed followers.
Ancient Human Kingdom Civilization that researched Will and ancestral soul structures before X's targeted genocide.
The Colony The Queen's soulless insectoid society and the Champions' restoration/progression hub.
Soulless Subjects Insectoid lives created by the Queen; cannot supply genuine Faith or be targeted by X's ordinary soul methods.
Champions Individuals selected and preserved by the Queen for her undisclosed purposes.

V. Oathbreaker — Character, Identity & Narrative

Keyword Definition
The Oathbreaker Knight of forgotten, illegitimate royal descent who caused the breach and seeks atonement.
The Oath His sworn duty to protect his hometown and its people.
The Breach Incident Crisis in which the Queen preserves the living knight, the Archivist manifests as the sword, and the knight's actions cause catastrophe.
Atonement His pursuit of redemption while insisting his oath was not willingly abandoned.
Forgotten Royal Descent His unknown connection to an illegitimate branch of the ancient royal family.
The Silent Family Retained relatives who silently polish his helmet and maintain his sword out of love or attachment, without granting Faith.
People He Helped Individuals aided during his knighthood; their gratitude does not grant him Faith or supernatural power.
Hidden Face His choice never to remove his helmet where others can see him, despite being physically able to.
The Sword's Manifestation The Archivist's response to the Queen's Preservation Condition superseding his pending Retention.
The Forgotten Heir [Descriptive / not official] Possible epithet referring to his concealed royal ancestry.

VI. Oathbreaker — Gameplay & Combat Vocabulary

Keyword Definition
Invocation Channeling an authorized ancestral soul's technique or ability through the Soulbound Sword.
Signature Weapon Slot Dedicated equipment/card interface keeping the Soulbound Sword reliably accessible outside normal draws.
Hex Board Tactical battlefield used for movement, positioning, and card-based abilities.
Draw Pile Standard card deck; the signature sword is not dependent on drawing it.
Deck Bloat Late-game deck growth that can reduce access to specific cards, avoided for the signature sword.
Will Mastery Growth in the Oathbreaker's self-conviction and efficiency of inherited powers, not unrestricted soul access.
Roguelike Rebirth Loop Party defeat followed by the Queen restoring previously preserved Champions at the colony.

VII. Historical Events & Timeline

Keyword Definition
Precursor Rift Rare ancient passage through which the Archivist encountered the early human kingdom; distinct from modern Rifts.
Revelation of Will The Archivist's teaching of an already-existing spiritual principle to the ancient ruler.
Genocide of Will Practitioners X's deliberate extermination of the kingdom and those who taught or protected Will during the first modern Rift chaos.
Activation of the Veil The kingdom's last-hour, untested spiritual contingency after X's attack was already underway.
The Breach Catastrophe The Oathbreaker's personally caused disaster that killed his family and made him an oathbreaker.

VIII. Foundational Keywords at a Glance

Principle Narrative role
Faith Supernatural amplification monopolized by X / the Coalition.
Will Independent self-conviction, and the ideological threat to X.
Retention The Archivist's eternal custody without rebirth.
Preservation The Queen's pre-death protection of restorability.
Sovereign Veil Concealment and bloodline restriction safeguarding the lost inheritance.

Terminology safeguards

  • The Library is the Archivist's older, pre-Veil collection; the Ancestral Domain is the concealed inheritance associated with the kingdom.
  • The Accord is the interworld alliance; X / the Coalition is the antagonistic group manipulating it.
  • Ancestral Dominion commands eligible souls, but does not produce or receive Faith.
  • Preservation occurs while alive; Restoration can occur after death only for already-preserved souls.
  • The Queen's insects can observe the material world but cannot see through the Sovereign Veil.
