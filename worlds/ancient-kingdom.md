An early civilization that cultivated [[Spiritus]] and was destroyed through the [[Stewards]]’ machinations.

## From the current canon

> The Stewards detected the Ancient Kingdom's Spiritus as a threat to their soul-exploitation system. They <strong>did not manipulate or redirect Rifts themselves</strong>. Instead, they manipulated the kingdoms and civilizations entering through the Rifts, exploited the chaos of first contact, falsely blamed the Ancient Kingdom, and directed the resulting hostility toward it. The Stewards <strong>orchestrated the Kingdom's destruction through these civilizations</strong> rather than attacking it directly. The first Rift members subsequently founded the Accord upon the Kingdom's ruins. The precise identities of participating civilizations and their individual grievances remain to be developed. The Stewards were <strong>unaware of the Wyrm</strong>. The Wyrm did not care that the Kingdom fell.
