Plot-hole and continuity audit

Type Issue Current handling
Open How did the royal bloodline survive the Kingdom's destruction and lose knowledge of its origins? Survival and Oathbreaker's descent are established; chain of descent is not.
Open What exactly happened during the father's debt crisis immediately before the Codex manifested? Human-scale event sequence remains to be written.
Open How does the Wyrm acquire a soul's knowledge while leaving Sanctum and Sovereignty intact? Result is established; access mechanism is undefined.
Open How does a pact by the King apply to descendants not yet born without compromising Sovereignty? Bloodline Testament remains established; precise metaphysics need care.
Open Do Transmogrified souls retain consciousness and Sanctum? Can Siphoning gradually consume a soul? Neither mechanic finalized.
Open — memory gap Why does the Oathbreaker not remember making his Binding Contract with the Wyrm? The contract, and that it preceded the Codex's first manifestation, are established; the cause of the lost memory is not.
Undefined mechanic How did the Oathbreaker's Codex contract persist through his death and Restoration? Death, Restoration and the continuing contract are established; how the contract carried over is not.
Undefined mechanic What is the relationship between the Library and the Ancestral Domain? Inscripture places souls in the Library, and the Oathbreaker's Inscribed ancestors are reached through the Ancestral Domain; whether the Domain is part of the Library or separate is not defined.
Open — competing claims Does the Bloodline Testament keep any claim on the Oathbreaker's soul now that he is Preserved? Preservation takes precedence over Inscripture at death; whether the Testament's pledge lapses, waits, or still has any effect is not defined.
Plot-hole risk If elite Stewards can forcibly breach Sanctums, why did they destroy the Kingdom through other civilizations instead of attacking it themselves? The indirect destruction is established; the Stewards' reason for choosing it is not.
Continuity risk How do the Stewards remain unaware of the Wyrm after the Codex's town-sized catastrophe? The Stewards' ignorance of the Wyrm is established; whether they noticed the catastrophe, and why it did not lead them to the Wyrm, is not.
Intentional mystery What is the game, and why does the Queen's Preservation subvert it? Do not prematurely explain.
Intentional mystery Where do unclaimed souls go after death? Unknown by design.
