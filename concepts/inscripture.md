The [[Wyrm]]’s post-death acquisition of [[Soul|souls]] into the [[Library]].

## From the current canon

> <strong>Inscripture</strong> incorporates a soul's intact consciousness into the Library for the Wyrm's knowledge collection. Inscribed souls retain identity, memories, consciousness, Sanctum and Sovereignty. They remain conscious, can interact with other souls in their respective collections, and can refuse requests. They cannot leave their collections under their own power or be Restored. Their experience resembles a shared afterlife, though their confinement remains real. No forced dormancy is canon.
