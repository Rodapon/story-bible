The protective boundary around a [[Soul|soul]] that guards against unauthorized access.

## From the current canon

> <strong>Sanctum</strong> protects a soul's <strong>Sovereignty</strong> (its authority over itself). <strong>Spiritus</strong> is <em>the innate ability to strengthen one's own Sovereignty</em>. Strong Spiritus increases resistance to Siphoning and manipulation, including when Sanctum is lowered, but is not immunity and does not strengthen the Sanctum itself.
