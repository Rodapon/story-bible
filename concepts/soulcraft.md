The [[Stewards]]’ innate capacity to manipulate, extract, reshape, and consume [[Soul|souls]].

## From the current canon

> <strong>Soulcraft</strong> is the Stewards' innate capacity to extract, manipulate, reshape and consume souls. <strong>Siphoning</strong> extracts spiritual essence; <strong>Transmogrification</strong> can turn souls into physical objects; <strong>Consumption</strong> uses souls as sustenance, with complete Consumption permanently destroying a soul. Exact mechanics and limits of partial Consumption and Transmogrified consciousness remain open. Elite Stewards may forcibly breach Sanctums; ordinary Stewards rely on lowered Sanctums.
